ABOUT ME

WHO AM I?

I am Likhon Chandra Dutta, an independent filmmaker and video editor from Chittagong. I have had a deep passion for films since childhood. Notable works and achievements: The World of Satyajit Ray (2021): I made a one-hour documentary on the occasion of the 100th birth anniversary of legendary filmmaker Satyajit Ray, in which I worked as a writer, director and producer. Eighteen to Twenty: This is a special feature film project of mine, which I made entirely using smartphones. Creative philosophy: I believe that making political films is easy, but making films politically is the real challenge. Apart from this, I am a video editor. I especially use Adobe Premiere Pro for video editing. I even edited both "The World of Satyajit Ray" and "Eighteen to Twenty" myself
